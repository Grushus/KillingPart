Killing Part: feel prototype

A browser sketch of the core loop, made on Fri 13 Nov before the Unreal build. Its numbers are the starting tuning in DESIGN.md. It is a design reference, not game code.

Playable prototype of Killing Part: hold and drag to draw a chalk line from the seamstress, let go, and she dashes along it and snips every plush monster on the line in half.

Hold and drag to draw her cut. Time slows while you draw. Let go to snip.
