KILLING PART  (v1.1)
Made by omgxdlmao & Claude for the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam (theme: Director's Cut)

Your plushies have come alive, and they want to hug you to death.

HOW TO PLAY
- Hold the left mouse button: time slows down. Drag to draw your cut (the chalk is limited).
- Let go: she dashes along your line and cuts everything on it in half.
- Cut several plushies in one line for a combo (5 or more: "Killing part").
- Don't let them touch you: every hug costs a heart.
- Don't cut through pincushions: that costs a heart too.
After each wave, pick a card. Esc pauses the game.

Short on time? On the modes screen, Practice lets you play any night, Monday to Sunday.
Nothing is saved there.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.1
The game is the jam build. 1.1 adds the WeLoveIndies intro at launch (their licence
asks for it); click or press any key to skip it.

FIRST LAUNCH
The first launch compiles shaders for about 40 seconds ("Preparing..." in the corner).
It isn't frozen.

CONTENT NOTE
Cartoon violence against plushies, with cartoon blood and gore in the Director's cut.
The Theatrical cut has white stuffing and no blood. The game asks which one you want
at first launch, and you can switch any time from the menu or the pause screen.

SYSTEM
Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Mouse only. Your progress is saved in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\KillingPart\Saved\SaveGames
